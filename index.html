WHO WE ARE

Growing Seeds of Purpose

Hluma Mbewu Sikubone (HMS) is a faith-based NGO based in George, South Africa, dedicated to empowering teenage girls.

Our name reflects our heart: "Grow, Seed, So We Can See You." We believe every young girl carries potential, purpose and a future worth nurturing.

Through mentorship, school outreach and conferences, HMS creates a safe and supportive environment where girls can navigate peer pressure, develop resilience and grow into purposeful young leaders.

We don't just want young women to survive their environment. We want them to discover who they are, know their purpose and grow confidently in Christ.
